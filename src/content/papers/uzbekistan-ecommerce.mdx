In the summer of 2025 I was a research intern in Yandex's International Marketing Department in Moscow. The department wanted to know what barriers and opportunities Yandex would face if it entered e-commerce in Uzbekistan. I answered with three sources of evidence: interviews with 19 small-business owners using a guide I designed, a survey of more than 60 local consumers, and a benchmark of 8 e-commerce platforms, plus a revenue analysis of local competitors. The result was the report "E-commerce Market of Uzbekistan: Barriers and Opportunities," which the department adopted.

<p class="note">This page describes the method only. The report and its data belong to Yandex and aren't shown here.</p>

## The question

What barriers and opportunities would Yandex face if it entered e-commerce in Uzbekistan? The sources cover both sides of a marketplace: consumers, who would buy, and small-business owners, who would sell.

## What could be wrong with it

Nineteen interviews are enough to find the main barriers sellers name, but not to say how common each one is. A survey of about 60 people can show patterns, not market shares, and who answered depends on how the survey reached them. A benchmark of 8 platforms describes those platforms; a different set could change the comparison.
