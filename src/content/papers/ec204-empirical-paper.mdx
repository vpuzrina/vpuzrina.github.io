## The model

The paper estimates a cross-sectional OLS regression of an outcome on the regressor of interest and a set of controls:

$$
y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1\, x_i + Z_i'\gamma + \varepsilon_i
$$

Here $\beta_1$ is the association between $x_i$ and the outcome $y_i$ once the controls in $Z_i$ are held fixed.

## What could be wrong with it

A single cross-section shows an association, not by itself a cause. A variable we leave out that moves both the outcome and the regressor would bias $\beta_1$, and so would causation running the other way.
